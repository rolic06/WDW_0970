INFRASTRUCTURE OF ABESEC

WHY TO CHOOSE ABESEC?

• Let me tell you about ABES Engineering College, a prominent hub of technical education located right on the Delhi-NCR border.

. As you drive down the NH-24 (Delhi-Meerut Expressway) in Ghaziabad, you absolutely cannot miss its imposing presence.

• The moment you step through the main gates, you are greeted by a massive, meticulously manicured green campus.

• It spans across several acres, seamlessly blending modern concrete architecture with vibrant natural landscapes.

• I always feel a sense of structured discipline the moment I walk past the security gates and into the main driveway.

• The sprawling lawns out front are perfectly geometric, instantly giving off a premium institutional vibe.
• You will notice that the entrance is always buzzing with energetic students rushing to their morning lectures.

• The architectural layout is highly organized, divided into distinct academic blocks named after great scholars and scientists.

• Every corner of the campus feels intentionally designed to foster a focused learning environment.
• It stands as a landmark in the Vijay Nagar region, highly accessible to students traveling from Delhi, Noida, and Greater Noida.
